I built the web presence for Reto MD, a mobile app that prepares Mexican physicians for the ENARM residency exam. I consolidated the specialty websites into one multi-tenant application with App Router and next-intl: each specialty’s branding, content, and features are configuration-driven, removing the need to duplicate projects.
