Vallarta TOURS is the fictional brand I use to showcase a website I originally built for a boat tour operator in Puerto Vallarta. Built with Next.js, React, TypeScript, and next-intl, it presents five tours, experience details, a booking flow, and contact information in Spanish and English, with localized page URLs so visitors keep their place when they switch languages.
