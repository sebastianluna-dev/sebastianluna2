I turned my experience as a chess coach into a platform that brings my academy’s teaching, study, and training into one digital experience. Powered by Next.js, React, and TypeScript, it combines courses and classes with game databases, a move trainer, puzzles, and analysis boards, with role-based access for students, instructors, and administrators.
