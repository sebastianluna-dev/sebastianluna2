I designed and built Cuarteto Fratres’ website to showcase their music and support bookings for concerts, weddings, and private events. Built with Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Payload CMS on PostgreSQL, it brings together upcoming performances, musician profiles, repertoire, and contact, and the group manages content and booking inquiries independently.
